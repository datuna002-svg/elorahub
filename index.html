Get better answers

Six habits that make any AI more useful

  • Say the goal, not just the task. "Rewrite this so my manager approves the budget" beats "rewrite this".
  • Paste the exact error. Include the message and the code around it — not a description of it.
  • Name the format. "A table", "three bullet points" or "a full Python file".
  • Give constraints. Budget, deadline, language version, what you've already tried.
  • Ask for options. "Give me three different approaches and the trade-offs" gets past the first idea.
  • Push back. "Why?", "are you sure?" and "shorter" are good follow-ups, not rude ones.
FAQ

Straight answers

Is elora free?

Yes — the Free plan includes a daily message allowance and file uploads. Private and Premium give you more messages; Premium is unlimited. See Pricing for details.

Which AI models does it use?

Leading open-weight reasoning models (served by Groq) and Google's Gemini. elora picks automatically and switches if one is busy, so you get an answer instead of an error.

Does it remember me?

Only when you're signed in, and only a short note of stable facts like your name and ongoing projects. You can read it or erase it anytime in Settings → Memory.

Can it browse the web?

It can read links you paste and run a search for questions about current events. It can't log in to sites or take actions on your behalf.

What files can I attach?

Images (up to 3 MB) and text or source-code files (up to 200 KB; elora reads the first ~30,000 characters). Other types, like PDFs and Word documents, are attached by name only for now.

Where do my messages go?

To elora's AI provider to write your reply. Your recent chats are saved in your browser. Card payments are handled by Bank of Georgia — elorahub never sees your card number.

Begin on the Free plan, move to Private when you need more room, and go unlimited on Premium whenever you're ready.

      Using elora well

      elora is built to reason, not just autocomplete. You'll get sharper answers if you treat it like a capable colleague rather than a search box.

      • Say what you actually want the answer to do — inform a decision, draft something final, or just help you think.
      • Give real constraints (budget, audience, deadline). elora weighs trade-offs better with something concrete to weigh.
      • Push back on an answer if it's off. elora will re-reason from your correction rather than just repeating itself.
      • For bigger jobs, switch on Task. elora plans the steps, works through them one by one, and hands you the files.

      Uploading files & images

      You can attach files and images to any conversation — drag them in, or press Ctrl+U. How many uploads each plan includes is listed on the Pricing page.

      • Images up to 3 MB, and text or source-code files up to 200 KB — elora reads the first ~30,000 characters of each.
      • PDFs, Word documents and other formats are attached by name only for now. Paste the text if you need elora to read it.
      • Only upload files you have the right to share — don't include others' private or confidential data without permission.
      • Larger files may take longer for elora to fully read before it starts reasoning about them.

      Acceptable use

      elorahub is for genuine work, learning, and creativity. To keep it useful and safe for everyone, don't use elora to:

      • Generate content intended to harass, deceive, or harm a real person or group.
      • Attempt to extract another user's private data or bypass account and usage limits.
      • Produce malicious code, or material intended to facilitate illegal activity.
      Accounts that repeatedly violate these guidelines may be limited or suspended. See the full for details.

      Where elora falls short

      elora reasons carefully, but it isn't infallible. Treat it as a very capable second opinion, not a final authority — especially for medical, legal, or financial decisions, where you should confirm anything important with a qualified professional.

      Reporting an issue

      If elora gives you a response that seems wrong, unsafe, or off-brand for what we're building, press the thumbs-down under that reply, or email elorahubonline@gmail.com. Real examples help us improve answer quality fastest.

      Fix a problem

      The things people run into most

      elora says it's busy or couldn't answer

      elora already switches between several AI models when one is overloaded, but now and then all of them are busy at once. Wait a few seconds and press Try again under the message.

      I've run out of messages

      The Free plan has a daily limit that resets each day. Private and Premium give you much more room — check what's left in Settings → Usage, or compare plans on the Pricing page.

      elora didn't read my file

      Images over 3 MB and text files over 200 KB are skipped, and PDFs or Word documents are attached by name only for now. Paste the important part as text and elora will work with it.

      A task stopped halfway

      Each step of a task is its own request, so a busy moment can interrupt it. Press Run the task again under the task and elora starts it fresh.

      elora lost track of something said earlier

      Very long chats are trimmed to the most recent messages so replies stay fast. Start a new chat with a short summary of what matters, and keep memory on so elora remembers the basics about you.

      The site looks out of date or broken

      Do a hard refresh to load the newest version — Ctrl + Shift + R on Windows, ⌘ + Shift + R on a Mac.

      Privacy

      elorahub only uses your data to run the service. It's never sold and never used to train AI models. Read the for the details.

      How we protect your data
      • Everything between you and elorahub travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
      • Sign-in is handled by Supabase — elorahub never sees your password.
      • Data saved to your account is locked so only you, and the server acting for you, can read it.
      • Card details go straight to Bank of Georgia. elorahub never sees or stores your card number.
      • Chats are kept in this browser and, when you're logged in, synced to your account.
      How we use your data
      • Your messages go to elora's AI providers (Groq and Google Gemini) only to write the reply.
      • If Memory is on, elora keeps short facts you share so answers fit you better. Turn it off any time.
      • Message counts are used to apply your plan's limits.
      • elorahub doesn't sell your data or use your chats to train AI models.
      • You can export everything below, or ask us to delete your account.
